The **AI Dashboard** (`/dashboard`) provides an interactive analytics workspace designed to be tailored to each user's visual reporting preferences.

---

## 1. Interactive Grid Capabilities

Every analytics card supports flexible spatial manipulation:
- **Draggable Reordering**: Grab any card by its header to rearrange card placement on the screen.
- **2D Corner Resizing**: Drag the bottom-right corner handle to expand or shrink card dimensions horizontally and vertically.
- **Auto-Persist Layout**: Size and position states are automatically saved to local storage, preserving your dashboard setup across sessions.
- **Lock Layout**: Click the padlock icon in the dashboard toolbar to freeze positioning during client demos and meetings.

---

## 2. Floating AI Copilot Canvas

For contextual on-the-fly inquiries into dashboard metrics:
- Toggle the **Floating Copilot Canvas** from the bottom-right action trigger.
- The overlay panel provides side-by-side AI analysis of your active card widgets without navigating away from the dashboard.

---

## 3. Responsive Display Adaptation

- **Desktop & Widescreen**: Multi-column spatial layout with full drag-and-drop and resizing.
- **Mobile & Tablet**: Smoothly transforms into an uncluttered single-column vertical stack.
